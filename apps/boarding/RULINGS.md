# Boarding Actions: rulings

Where the books contradict each other or are silent, the app makes a choice. The
choices are listed here so that each can be disputed.

Sources: **RB** = Rulebook v1.10, **FL** = Fleets Book WiP v0.36, **REF** = the
two-page *Battlefleet Gothic — Boarding Actions* reference sheet, **FAQ** = the
Collected FAQ 2026 (wins over RB and FL; cited by its heading). Printed page =
PDF page. Key pages: boarding RB pp. 89-91, End Phase p. 88, crippled p. 65,
criticals pp. 66-67, catastrophic damage p. 71, defence criticals p. 101,
hit-and-run and teleports p. 92.

1. **The crippled +2 counts for every crippled enemy ship.** REF's example 3
   counts two crippled Kill Kroozers separately (+4); only that reading gives its
   printed total of 11. Blast markers and special orders count **once**, since no
   source says otherwise.
2. **REF's example 2 leaves out the ratio modifier** (11 vs 10 gives +1). The app
   follows the rule text, RB p. 90, not the example: the example's right result
   is Stormed, not Driven Back.
3. **REF's example 3 prints the Orks' boarding value as 8**, though 5 + 4 = 9. The
   example's own damage needs 9: a misprint.
4. **Tau halve rounding up.** The books do not say; every other halving in RB
   (pp. 65, 88) rounds up.
5. **A crippled defender adds half its turrets** (rounded up). RB p. 65 halves
   turrets, and what is added is "remaining turrets".
6. **The Mark of Khorne's +1 to cause criticals applies to every Khorne ship**
   (REF). FL prints the sentence only for the Incursion list's Warmaster.
7. **Berzerker Tide's D3 extra rolls** use the same threshold as the critical
   itself. Neither book says which; the app says so in the result.
8. **Tyranid spore cysts count as the defender's turrets** (REF and its example 3),
   added **after** the doubling.
9. **A boarding Tyranid always gets the blast-marker modifier**, because boarding
   itself puts a blast marker on the target (FL p. 418). The enemy never gets it
   from the Tyranid's own markers.
10. **The Inquisitor's +2 does not add to the Space Marine bonus** (FL p. 144's
    Grey Knights clause, extended to all three Ordos).
11. **Hexagrammic wards** (Ordo Malleus or Grey Knights) cancel both the Mark of
    Nurgle's protection against boarding and the Mark of Khorne's doubling.
12. **A ship without crew gets no crew modifiers.** An activated Blackstone
    Fortress "has no crew and so does not add D6" (FL p. 284). The app also leaves
    out its race bonus and crew upgrades; its boarding value and the modifiers from
    the opponent's state stay. The book does not say this directly, but +1 Chaos
    for a crewless artefact would plainly be wrong.
13. **A space hulk is never crippled** (FL pp. 288, 391): it gives no +2 and does
    not halve its turrets. **Tyranid spore cysts keep their full strength** when
    crippled (FL p. 424), unlike turrets.
14. **Race tables are used:** criticals for Eldar (FL p. 305), Dark Eldar (p. 344),
    Tyranids (p. 423), Necrons (p. 401), the Ork hulk (p. 391), the Chaos hulk
    (p. 288), the Ramilies (p. 505), the Eldar Haven (p. 337) and defences
    (RB p. 101); catastrophic damage for Tyranids (p. 423), hulks (pp. 288, 391)
    and the Ramilies (p. 505). The Kroot Warsphere, the Nicassar Caravan and the
    Rok roll neither: a critical is +1 damage and destruction is four blast markers.
15. **Boarding damage causes no extra criticals** (REF), and a ship brought to 0 by
    it is a drifting hulk without a catastrophic roll, **unless** the critical's
    extra damage took it to zero, when the catastrophic table is rolled (RB p. 90).
    RB p. 66's general sentence ("a check for critical damage is made for every
    point of damage scored on a ship, no matter what its source is") would argue
    for extra criticals. FAQ 2026 (*Boarding Actions: do you roll for critical
    hits…*) settles it the app's way: one critical for each ship with its own die,
    none from the loser's damage points.
16. **One ship can board several enemy ships.** Confirmed by the authors on
    13 Sept 2026: resolve it as a normal multi-boarding. Each side's boarding values
    are added, each side rolls once, the losing side's owner divides the damage
    among its ships, and a critical is checked for every ship involved. It works
    the other way too, several ships boarding one.
17. **The holofield save is per ship.** FL pp. 303-304: the save is rolled once
    against each attack by the ship attacked. In a multi-boarding each defending
    holofield hull rolls its own save; a successful save takes that ship out of
    this boarding (no boarding value, no damage, no critical), and if every
    defender saves, the attempt does not happen. The books do not cover several
    defenders; this is the closest reading of the rule's own words.
18. **A space hulk gets no Khorne doubling but does get Abaddon.** FL p. 288 denies
    hulks the Marks of Chaos and every upgrade that improves boarding (also
    p. 390), so the Mark of Khorne is blocked in the engine. Abaddon is a real
    contradiction: the same ban would cover him, but REF's example 4 counts a Chaos
    Space Hulk **with Abaddon** at 86 (40 × 2 + 6). The app follows REF, the only
    source that takes a position.
19. **Turrets and spore cysts can be adjusted by hand.** RB p. 89 counts the
    *remaining* turrets, and lists buy more (Ork Dakka Dakka FL p. 365, Mechanicus
    escorts +5 pts FL p. 121, extra Tyranid spore cysts FL p. 426) while criticals
    take them away. The profile's number is the starting value; the card marks a
    changed one with a star. The Explorator list's **mandatory** +1 turret is part
    of the big ships' price (FL p. 119), so it is already in the ship data. It
    does not apply to escorts (FL p. 121) or to the Omnissiah's Victory, which takes
    no refits (FL p. 126).
20. **A star fort's other quadrants are entered by hand.** FL p. 502: the boarding
    value counts the remaining hits of all four quadrants, but only the boarded
    quadrant's turrets. The data has one quadrant, so the others' hits are their
    own control, added **only** to the boarding value: not to the crippled test,
    the turrets or the damage.
21. **A hull belongs to the fleet that crews it.** The book's fleet lists take
    hulls from each other's sections, and in boarding the crew decides: the
    Explorator list's Imperial and Ordo hulls are Mechanicus ships and get no Space
    Marine bonus (FL pp. 121, 125), renegade hulls are Chaos ships in every
    respect (FL p. 222), rapid strike vessels are Imperial hulls crewed by the
    Chapter (FL p. 106), the Ordo Malleus and Segmentum Obscurus stock hulls are
    Chaos profiles with Imperial crews (FL pp. 39, 142, 15), and the Rogue Trader
    lists' hulls are the rogue trader's own (FL pp. 174, 178). The app therefore
    lists the same hull under several fleets. Allied contingents (Inquisition,
    Rogue Traders, Kroot, Demiurg, Nicassar) stay under their own fleet, and so
    do Demiurg Bastion vessels hired as mercenaries (FL p. 483) and the planetary
    defences every list can buy (FL p. 499), which stay in the defence fleets of
    their own chapter. The Imperial and Chaos escorts a Blood Axe tiddla skwadron
    commandeers are crewed by the Blood Axes and listed under Orks (FL p. 369).
    A variant profile of the same ship (the Haven as a pirate base, the Rok as a
    defence) is listed once: in a boarding action it is the same ship.
22. **A renegade hull cannot take Marks or Chaos Space Marine crew.** FL p. 222
    bars them from Chaos Lords, Chaos Space Marines and Chaos ordnance, and p. 225
    says a Mark always needs a Lord or CSM crew. They get only the +1 for Chaos,
    and the app hides Marks and crew upgrades from them.
