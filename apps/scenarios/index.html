About the texts and abbreviations

Every scenario here is summarised in our own words for play at the table; “Playing with other fleets” notes marked as suggestions are ours, not the source’s. Credit belongs to the original authors; the page references point to the sources: the BFG Remastered Rulebook v1.10 and Fleets v0.36, the classic Games Workshop and Fanatic publications (Warp Storm, Planet Killer, Battlefleet Gothic Magazine, Invasion, Fanatic Magazine, Fanatic Online) the fan magazine Warp Rift, and the fan supplements Book of Nemesis, Project Distant Darkness, Starblade, Book of the Void I and II, the BFG Tournament Mission Pack and BFG Advanced Scenarios.

RB = Rulebook v1.11, FL = Fleets v0.40.

Keyboard: 1–4 switch a scenario’s tabs · ← → change the turn on Play.
